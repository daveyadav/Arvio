How We Build

The Arvio
Process

Six stages, one accountable team. No subcontractor hands the next one a surprise — we walk every site from survey to handover.

01 —

Consultation
& Survey

Week 1–2

We meet on-site to understand the brief, walk the plot, and run a full topographic and soil survey before a single line is drawn.

02 —

Design
& Engineering

Week 3–7

Our architects and structural engineers develop drawings together, so the design that looks right is also the design that stands up.

03 —

Permits
& Approvals

Week 6–10

We file and track every municipal permit and structural approval ourselves, so your project never stalls in someone else's inbox.

04 —

Site Preparation
& Foundation

Week 10–16

Clearing, excavation, and a foundation poured to the soil report — not a standard spec — because no two sites carry a load the same way.

05 —

Construction
& Fit-Out

Month 4–11

Structural frame, MEP rough-in, façade, and finishing — run by one site supervisor you can call, not a rotating cast of foremen.

06 —

Inspection
& Handover

Final 2 Weeks

A third-party quality inspection, a full walkthrough with you, and a two-year structural warranty the day you get the keys.
