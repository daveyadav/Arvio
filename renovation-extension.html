Services/Renovation & Extension

Renovation & Extension

Structural additions and full renovations that respect what's already standing — from a single extra floor to a complete gut renovation.

Overview

Work that starts with what the existing structure can carry.

Before we touch a wall, our structural engineers assess what the existing building can actually support — critical for adding a floor or opening up a load-bearing wall in an older structure. That assessment shapes the design, not the other way around.

We sequence work to keep the property livable or operating wherever possible, with a single site supervisor coordinating trades so an extension doesn't turn into a year of disruption.

What's Included

Every renovation contract covers this

No hidden phases — these are priced into the contract from day one.

01

Existing-Structure Assessment

A structural engineer evaluates the current foundation and frame before any addition is designed.

02

Renovation Drawings

Plans that show exactly what's being kept, removed, and added, reviewed with you before work begins.

03

Permits & Approvals

We file every renovation and addition permit directly with the municipal authority on your behalf.

04

Structural Tie-In

New framing tied into the existing structure to current seismic standards, not just the old code.

05

Finishing & Fit-Out

New work finished to match or intentionally contrast with the existing property, your call either way.

06

Third-Party Inspection

An independent quality inspection before handover, plus a two-year structural warranty on new work.
