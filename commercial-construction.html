Overview

A building engineered for how the business will run.

Every commercial project starts with occupancy load, circulation, and code compliance — not a rendering. We coordinate architecture, structural, and MEP engineering from the first drawing, so the building that looks right in the pitch deck is also the building that passes inspection.

You get one project lead across design and construction, a phased schedule that tracks your opening date, and a fixed-price contract built from the structural and soil survey, not a placeholder estimate.

What's Included

Every commercial contract covers this

No hidden phases — these are priced into the contract from day one.

01

Site & Structural Survey

Topographic survey and geotechnical soil report sized for commercial loads before design is finalized.

02

Architecture & Engineering

Floor plans, structural drawings, and MEP coordination developed together, not handed off in sequence.

03

Permits & Code Compliance

We file every municipal permit, fire safety, and occupancy approval directly with the local authority.

04

Structural Frame & MEP

Seismic-rated RCC frame, elevators, HVAC, fire systems, and electrical infrastructure, built in-house.

05

Interior Fit-Out

Base-build and tenant fit-out coordinated together, so occupancy isn't waiting on a second contractor.

06

Third-Party Inspection

An independent quality and life-safety inspection before handover, plus a two-year structural warranty.
